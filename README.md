# simplyhangman

Think you have a way with words? Put your vocabulary to the test with Simply Hangman — the classic word guessing game, reimagined for your phone.

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the hangman is complete. With over 1,300 carefully chosen words spanning 14 categories — from Animals and Science to History, Space, and the Arts — every round brings a fresh challenge.

Features

• 1,300+ words across 14 categories
• Helpful hints when you’re stuck
• Track your wins, losses, and winning streak
• Clean minimal design with full dark mode support
• Play anywhere — no internet or data connection required
• Minimal, unobtrusive ads that never interrupt your game
• Completely free to play

Whether you’re killing time on your commute, sharpening your vocabulary, or just looking for a quick brain workout, Simply Hangman delivers a satisfying challenge every time. No wifi? No problem. Simply Hangman works perfectly on a plane, underground, or anywhere else life takes you.

Simple to pick up. Hard to put down.
